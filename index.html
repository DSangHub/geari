The 16-pin port is SAE J1962 / ISO 15031-3. The law’s name changes: OBDBr-2 in Brazil, EOBD in Chile and ASEAN, JOBD in Japan, KOBD in Korea, China 6, India BS-VI. GEARI reads RPM and speed, then infers gear.
