Connected to the car

The stick-shift computer
your phone never had.

Most telematics apps treat a clutch like an automatic. GEARI infers gear from the speed–RPM ratio, times the shift window, and scores how cleanly you complete it.

01

Plug in. Pair. Drive.

Bluetooth OBD-II or native CAN where supported. GEARI reads RPM, vehicle speed, throttle, and engine load. No dealer tool.

02

Every shift is a record

Up, down, skip-shift, or into reverse. Each event stores gear in, gear out, RPM band, jerk, and a smoothness grade.

03

Points for control

Smooth clutch work, signaled lane changes, and progressive braking add points. Dumping the clutch or stabbing the pedal costs them.

South America · Far East · same plug

Connect your car.

GEARI does not need a US-only OBD-II stack. The 16-pin port under the dash is the same in Brazil, Japan, India, and Thailand. The law has another name — OBDBr, EOBD, JOBD, KOBD, China 6, BS-VI. The app reads RPM, speed, and load from that port, then infers every gear.

01

Find the port

16-pin socket within reach of the driver — usually under the dash, driver side. Same SAE J1962 / ISO 15031-3 shape in every first-wave market.

02

Plug a BLE adapter

Bluetooth Low Energy dongle. iPhone needs BLE or MFi. Android accepts BLE or Classic. Cheap Classic clones will not pair on iOS.

03

Handshake

GEARI auto-detects CAN, KWP2000, or K-line. A ten-second smoke test asks for RPM and speed. Both streaming means ECU mode is on.

04

Fallback

No live PIDs? Phone GPS + IMU still scores lane and brake. The public rating shows a sensor badge, not a fake ECU score.

What the adapter talks to

One connector. Regional names.

Pins 6 and 14 carry CAN on most 2008+ / OBDBr-2 / China 6 / BS-VI cars. Older Asia and Europe use the K-line on pin 7. GEARI only needs legislated Mode 01 PIDs — not dealer coding.

SA

South America

Brazil OBDBr-2 / 2+ (ISO 15031, flex-fuel). Argentina and Chile follow EOBD-style ports on later cars. Mexico ~2007. Probe older Andean fleets — the hole can exist without live data.

FE

Far East

JOBD (Japan), KOBD (Korea), GB 18352 China 6, India BS-VI, Thailand and Philippines Euro 4 EOBD. JDM cars can be picky with clone dongles. Export models are usually easier.

PID

What we read

RPM, vehicle speed, throttle, calculated load, MAP or MAF. Enough to time a shift and grade jerk. Not fuel trim — ethanol flex cars swing those numbers.

HW

Adapter allow-list

Vgate iCar Pro BLE, Veepeak BLE+, OBDLink MX+ / CX. Avoid unlabeled ELM “v2.1” clones on Mercado Libre, Shopee, and Lazada for daily scoring.
